| ID | Sev | Finding | Status | Resolution / where tracked |

| CR-17 | LOW | Guidance cone was visibly jittery during real-world use, though it still guided correctly to the car. Likely cause: the cone's centerline bearing is computed from the raw, instantaneous compass heading each update; raw magnetometer readings are inherently noisy, especially with little device motion to aid sensor fusion. Cosmetic, not a correctness defect -- consistent with the project's "incomplete, not wrong" bar. | Open | Recommended direction (not yet implemented): apply a smoothing/low-pass filter to `device_heading` before it feeds the bearing formula (REQ-GUIDE-01), rather than rendering the raw instantaneous reading. | CR-18 | HIGH | Premature PARKED declaration on a brief low-speed stop before actually parking. Scenario: driving at highway speed (DRIVING, >25 mph) -> access road at 15-20 mph (dead zone, no transition, state remains DRIVING) -> a brief stop (<=5 mph) at a stop sign/entrance before pulling into the lot triggers PARKING -> if the stop lasts long enough for 3 samples to converge, PARKED is falsely declared -> the driver then creeps through the parking lot at low speed looking for a real spot, never exceeding `DRIVING_SPEED_THRESHOLD` (parking-lot speeds don't reach 25 mph), so REQ-PARK-05's drive-away invalidation never fires and the false location is never cleared.
